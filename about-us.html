Founded in 2022, Void Legacy is an independent urban clothing concept combining technical apparel, functional accessories and minimalist streetwear.

Our mission

To combine future-oriented digital style and robust, climate-conscious technical clothing for modern urban life.

Our vision

To become a leading high-concept techwear brand in Southern Africa through sustainable, functional design.

How we approach design

01 / Function

Purpose in every detail

Utility pockets, modular concepts and considered silhouettes guide the design process.

02 / Materials

Thoughtful sourcing

The proposal prioritises sustainable fabrics and durability. Material certifications and performance claims would need verification before commercial release.

03 / Identity

Room for expression

Designed for fashion lovers, digital artists and techwear enthusiasts aged 18–35 who value individual style.
